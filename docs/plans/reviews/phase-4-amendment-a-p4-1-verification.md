# Phase 4 amendment A-P4-1 — focused verification

1. **P4-A1-F1 — BLOCKING — date judging violates the no-regression rule.** Consider 100 present cells: 79 distinct ordinary dates, 20 occurrences of a built-in candidate date, and one non-date token. Before A-P4-1, 99/100 cells parse and the column is `datetime`; after candidate removal, only 79/80 parse, so datetime declines and the column can fall to `free_text`. Running after rules 0–4 protects constant and binary columns, as claimed, but not existing datetime, numeric, categorical, or later Phase 4 roles. This contradicts both the header’s sole decision-7 exception and the battery allowing only free-text-to-new-role/new-format transitions. Evidence: `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:52-77`, `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:1395-1404`, `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:1584-1605`; shipped downstream ordering: `src/synthtwin/taxonomy.py:3761-3804`. **Correction C1:** judge/remove date candidates only when the non-candidate remainder itself clears the datetime rule; otherwise do not enter the date-candidate pass. This preserves every pre-existing role without broadening the exception.

2. **P4-A1-F2 — BLOCKING — the date-stand-in wire contract is incomplete.** A producer following the amendment must emit a sixth absence-class key, date verdicts, and a third declaration list; a loader following P4-D7’s “complete” delta instead retains `missing_by_class` unchanged and has no permitted key, list name, value type, or ordering for them. The inherited verdict schema is explicitly numeric-only. Offset-bearing datetimes add another divergence: “matched as instants” does not settle whether candidate membership and day ordinals use the written calendar day or the normalized shared-clock day. Evidence: `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:1131-1193`, especially `:1169-1173`, against `:1584-1600`; inherited five-key and numeric-verdict invariants: `docs/spec/profile-contract-v4.md:588-603`, `:630-663`; two-list declaration shape: `docs/spec/profile-contract-v5.md:583-595`; offset semantics: `docs/spec/profile-contract-v4.md:993-999`. **Correction C2:** fix the sixth key’s exact spelling and all-six membership/sum/floor invariants; define the date verdict’s canonical candidate representation, mixed candidate ordering, reasons and withholding; name and type the third declaration-list key and update its count identity; specify local-versus-shared-clock candidate identity, reference population and ordinal calculation; then enumerate every contract, method, validator and shipped-code migration.

3. **P4-A1-F3 — SERIOUS — unpadded slashed-date membership has two conforming readings.** The amendment says “the two slashed date families,” while the amended Phase 4 table contains three date-only slashed families: inherited month-first and day-first plus `slashed-iso-date`. Thus an unpadded year-leading value can either parse or decline depending on the implementer. Evidence: `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:735-776`, `:1566-1569`; inherited formats: `docs/spec/profile-contract-v4.md:914-918`. **Correction C3:** enumerate by wire name exactly which date-only families accept one- or two-digit month/day fields and give their complete delimiter/year-width grammar. The compact-date family remains non-overlapping because it requires exactly eight digits with no separators (`src/synthtwin/parsing.py:897-903`).

4. **P4-A1-F4 — SERIOUS — disclosure and claim-migration pricing is incomplete.** Unpadded and slashed-datetime columns that formerly landed in `free_text` now publish datetime facts, including floor-free endpoints and ladder values. The amendment prices header calibration and format mechanics but does not add these newly exposed facts—or the date-declaration list—to P4-D7’s complete disclosure delta or claim-migration table. Evidence: `docs/plans/phase-4-columns.md:79-96`, `:182-189`, `:243-259`, `:1194-1243`, against `:1566-1600`; the inherited free-text publication boundary is `docs/spec/profile-contract-v4.md:1304-1312`. **Correction C4:** add disclosure rows with floor treatment plus SECURITY/summary consequences for both slashed widenings and the date-declaration list; add their moving claims to P4-D7.9; amend the stale scope, decision-4 cardinality, and P4-D0 status sentences.
