### What C6-ARG destroys if it is transcribed as drafted

C6-ARG supersedes "version 4's §4.5 note grammar for its forms, arities and argument classes" (v6:1117-1118), and §2.2.2A rules each superseded item "superseded ENTIRE and replaced by this document's own statement of it" (v6:142). The only sentence binding the 38 forms is v4:424-426, which names `taxonomy.NOTE_ARITY` and the three argument classes. Once it goes, a version 6 document's `detection_evidence`, `remarks` and `source.header_evidence` are bound by nothing, the six publication-note forms are bound by nothing, and the word class has no members, while C6-ARG's replacement names three `remark_*` forms. A conforming version 6 implementation would have no authority to publish the sentence that says a column is empty, or that labels were pooled, or which reading the header verdict took. The reviewer's item P4-X5-F11 is correct, and its size is 38 inherited forms — plus the extensions and the new form the plan requires under G3, which C6-GRAMMAR does not yet carry.
